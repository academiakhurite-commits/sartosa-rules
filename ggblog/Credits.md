### Works Cited

- Araby Smugglers based on Araby Tomb Raiders warband by Khemri Development Team, headed by Tommy Muller, Brian Coggin & Terry Maltman. For complete list of contributors see 'Khemri: The Land of the Dead' document.
- 'Araby Merchant' hero based on Hired Sword by Nicodemus Kyme
- Cargo Table based on Cargo from Scenario 'Down at the Docks,' by Mark Havener. The Scenario is also the basis for the Sartosa Scenario "Get the Booty!"
- Rules for Cargo Movement and Stashing based on the Scenario 'Gunpowder Plot', by Ryn Tyrr Guild.
- Great Coat & other Cathayan Items are from the 'Border Town Burning' Campaign by Christian "Cianty" Templin, Stu "Werekin" Cresswell, and the rest of the Border Town Burning Development team. For full credits, see http://www.bordertownburning.de.vu/
- Gas Mask/Air Mask, Sentries, Night, and Guards rules taken from 'Relics of the Crusade'.
- The Pirate skill 'Nimble' (renamed 'Quick as a Monkey') is taken from the Swashbuckler HS, by Daniel Logee.
- Random Events based on Random Happenings by Mark Havener and Mike Huckelbery.
- Scenarios 'King of the Hull' and 'Here Be Monsters' based on "Wizards Mansion" and "Monster's Lair" by Mark Havener, from 'Chaos on the Streets.'
- Scenario "Sneak Sneak" based on the Scenario "Unawares" by Ross "Pancreas Boy" Franks.
- Multiple scenarios, including "Last Orders" and "Island Hopping" taken from Nemesis Crown and Lustria campaign.
- Silver Bullets by Boss Umguk\Killer Shrike.
- Slayer Pirate Warband and ability 'Festooned with Pistols' adapted from the 'Slayer Pirate' Hired Sword by Tom 'Myntok' Gabel, the "Dwarf Treasure Hunter Warband" from Mark Havener, and the "Slayer Cult" Warband by Dave Joria.
- "Lothern Sea Patrol" Warband based on the Shadow Warrior Warband by Mark Havener and the Dark Elf Warband, by the Lustria iCircle gaming group. Magic List from "Elf Mage Hired Sword" by Jake Thornton.
- Swimming and Basic Boat Rules based on "Warhammer Ahoy" 2nd Ed. By Spirit of the Game. Additional 'Advanced Boat Rules' based on the 'Empire in Flames' Expansion, by Steve Hambrook, Nick Kyme, Mark Havener & Anthony Reynolds. For additional credits, see the Empire in Flames expansion set.
